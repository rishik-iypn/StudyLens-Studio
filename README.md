## What it does

- **Home** — A greeting, quick-start tiles, papers to mark and your recent papers in one place.
- **Exam Studio** — Eleven question types, sections, passages, timers, marks, lockdown exams and PDF import.
- **Live Quiz** — Host for everyone on the same Wi-Fi, with a countdown, points for speed and a leaderboard.
- **Classes** — Students join once with a code like `K7QM-2XRA`. Homework you set reaches the whole class.
- **Results** — Marks, letters, CBSE or IB markbands, marked later and published straight to students.
- **Surveys** — Anonymous ratings and agree scales, shown as charts.
- **Practice** — Make your own tests and quizzes and practise them as often as you like.
- **Lens Agent** — On-device AI that makes quizzes, hosts live quizzes and sets up classes for you.
- **Studio Themes** — Use any theme you design in the Studio Themes app.

It adapts to who you are: **Teacher**, **Student** or **Just me**.

## Install

**From MarkLens:** open **Settings › Studio Apps** and click **Get**. MarkLens installs it for you.

**Manually:**
1. Download `StudyLens-Studio-<version>.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/rishik-iypn/StudyLens-Studio/releases).
2. Unzip it and drag **StudyLens Studio** into **Applications**.
3. If macOS can’t verify the app, go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

Requires **macOS 26** or later.

## Works best with MarkLens Studio

StudyLens works on its own, but with [MarkLens Studio 2.0](https://github.com/TheCommandPrompt-Mac/MarkLens) you can write homework and notes, turn any note into a quiz with **Quiz from a Note**, and hand work in from your library. Get it from **Settings › Studio Apps**.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac and your classroom’s Wi-Fi. Quizzes, exams and hand-ins travel directly between Macs on the same network, never through a server.
