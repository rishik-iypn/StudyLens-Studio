## What it does

- **See every part** — Preview your theme in the MarkLens editor, the MarkLens library and a StudyLens quiz.
- **Edit everything** — Accent, fonts, headings, links, quotes, highlights, code, checklists, glass, grain, cursor, tabs, focus and night warmth.
- **Palettes** — Turn your accent into an Analogous, Complementary, Triadic, Pastel or Mono colour set in one click.
- **Compare and undo** — Hold to see the original, and undo or redo any change.
- **Readability score** — Know when text is hard to read, and fix it in one click.
- **Use it everywhere** — Send a theme to MarkLens Studio, StudyLens Studio or every app at once.

## Install

**From MarkLens:** open **Settings › Studio Apps** and click **Get**. MarkLens installs it for you.

**Manually:**

1. Download `Studio-Themes-<version>.zip` from [Releases](https://github.com/rishik-iypn/Studio-Themes/releases).
2. Unzip it and drag **Studio Themes** into **Applications**.
3. If macOS can’t verify the app, go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

Requires **macOS 26** or later. Works best with **MarkLens Studio 2.0** (join the beta in MarkLens › Settings › Beta Updates).

## Privacy

Your themes stay on your Mac. Studio Themes only uses the network to download Studio apps you ask for.
